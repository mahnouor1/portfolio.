Skimmy.ai, a voice AI receptionist

I started Skimmy for an Australian startup pitch, aimed at clinics and real estate agencies that miss calls. It's connected to real phone numbers through Twilio telephony, so it picks up actual calls, understands what the caller needs, answers their questions and books the appointment.

After every call, the conversation gets scored for intent, timeline and lead quality. It's logged to a sheet, and if the lead is hot, the owner gets a text.
